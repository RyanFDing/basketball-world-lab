# After Rain / Free Throw

Target established before modeling, 2026-09-07, 16:53 Pacific.

A grounded, realistic blue-hour outdoor court. A single athlete in a muted ivory practice jersey, dark teal shorts and off-white basketball shoes. Warm 3200 K court lighting shapes the arms and face; a cool sky gives readable fill. The basket sits beyond the athlete in a diagonal composition. The camera begins low and close to the shooting side, then eases toward the basket while preserving the full shot arc. Asphalt has aggregate and patched wear, with shallow irregular puddles concentrated near the perimeter. White markings remain readable. A transparent backboard, orange steel rim and fine hanging net are the second focal point. Fence, trees, modest skyline and lamps provide depth without competing with the shot.
