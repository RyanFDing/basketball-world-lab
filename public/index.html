ABOUT THIS STUDY

A virtual free throw.

This is a synthetic athlete with an authored animation. It does not reconstruct your shooting technique, validate biomechanics, or provide coaching advice.

Flight uses gravity at 9.81 m/s². Rim, finite backboard, support pad, and floor contacts use an approximate sphere collision model. The net response is cosmetic and does not affect the ball.

The ball is 0.238 m across; the upper rim surface is 3.048 m high with a 0.4572 m clear opening. Changing launch conditions preserves the release position; the body animation remains authored.

Drag in Orbit mode to rotate, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan. Space plays, R restarts, H toggles presentation, Esc exits presentation. Launch settings are saved in this browser. Save/Open transfers them as JSON.
